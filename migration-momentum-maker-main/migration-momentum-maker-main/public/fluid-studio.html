The Sculpt Pipeline — how units become real
In the forge · not yet filed
Sculpt high-poly in Blender / ZBrush against the concept sheets → retopologize to game-ready meshes → bake normal, AO, curvature → texture in Substance Painter (albedo / roughness / metallic / normal) → export glTF (.glb). The Asset Lab is the receiving surface: GLTFLoader, LOD switching, orbit inspection. Each exported unit converts its slot from BLOCKED_MISSING_ASSET to PROVEN — the pipeline is the resurrection, and every unit the Harvester unmade, we remade.
