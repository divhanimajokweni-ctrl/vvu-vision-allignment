# Partnership Proposal: Garmin Uncle (Pilot)

## Strategic Partnership Opportunity

VVU (Venture Vision Ubuntu) proposes a strategic partnership with Garmin Uncle (Pilot)
to co-develop and deploy sovereign digital twin technology in the health sector.

## Value Proposition
- Real-time digital twin of health operations
- Cryptographic proof of all state transitions (WORM ledger)
- Fail-closed architecture (no hallucinations)
- POPIA-compliant (no raw biometric storage)
- On-chain anchoring (Polygon) for public verifiability
