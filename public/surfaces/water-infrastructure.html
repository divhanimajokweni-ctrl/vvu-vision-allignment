Venture Vision Ubuntu (VVU) is a verification platform that watches water infrastructure — pipelines, pump stations, sensor pods — and records what is happening in one append-only history. Every important change is bound to a tamper-evident log: an append-only record chain sealed with SHA-256 hashes, so history cannot be quietly rewritten.
