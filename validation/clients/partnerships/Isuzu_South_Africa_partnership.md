# Partnership Proposal: Isuzu South Africa

## Strategic Partnership Opportunity

VVU (Venture Vision Ubuntu) proposes a strategic partnership with Isuzu South Africa
to co-develop and deploy sovereign digital twin technology in the automotive sector.

## Value Proposition
- Real-time digital twin of automotive operations
- Cryptographic proof of all state transitions (WORM ledger)
- Fail-closed architecture (no hallucinations)
- POPIA-compliant (no raw biometric storage)
- On-chain anchoring (Polygon) for public verifiability

## Revenue Model
- O2O tier: R119,988/year
- Revenue sharing: 70/30 (VVU/Partner) on new subscriptions
- White-label option available
